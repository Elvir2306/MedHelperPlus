The MedHelper+ app was created to serve as a personal health assistant, primarily designed to remind users to take their prescribed medication on schedule and to attend medical appointments.

I developed the app based on my own experience with daily medication routines. I took into account various situations a user might face when a medication reminder goes off—such as driving, not having the device nearby, or sleeping—and sought to make the app truly adaptable to these real-life scenarios. Consequently, MedHelper+ is not simply a clone of any existing similar app.
