Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 04, 2026

Elvir Đulan ("we," "us," or "our") develops the MojaTerapija mobile application (the "App"). Your privacy is our priority, and this document explains clearly how we handle your data.

1. Permissions & Functionality

To ensure your medication reminders are delivered reliably, the App requires the following Android permissions:

  • Notifications: To show alerts when it is time to take your medicine.
  • Exact Alarms: To trigger reminders at the precise minute you scheduled.
  • Battery Optimization: To prevent the Android system from pausing your alarms to save power.
  • Full-Screen Intent: To display the alarm window on your lock screen for immediate action.
  • Run at Startup: To automatically reschedule your reminders after you restart your phone.

2. Local Health Data

Strictly Private: All your medication names, dosages, schedules, and history logs are stored 100% locally on your device.

We do not use external servers to store your health information. We have no access to your data, and it never leaves your phone. Your information is cleared if you choose to wipe your history in settings or uninstall the App.

3. Usage Analytics

To improve the App's stability and identify technical bugs, we use Firebase Analytics (a service provided by Google).

  • Information Collected: Firebase collects non-identifiable technical data such as device model, app version, and crash reports.
  • Purpose: This helps us fix crashes and understand which features are most useful. It does not include your name, email, or medicine names.
  • Google’s Policy: You can learn more about how Google handles data here: Google Privacy Partners Page.

4. Advertising

MojaTerapija is 100% Ad-Free. We do not display third-party ads and we do not sell your data to advertisers.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Since data is stored locally, we have no way of identifying the age of our users.

6. Data Deletion & Control

You have total control over your data. You can delete individual logs, wipe your entire history, or remove all medications through the "Settings" menu at any time.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
